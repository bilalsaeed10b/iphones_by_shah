A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you pick.

What does the condition score mean?

Shah grades each individual device out of 10 in half-point steps. It is a manual assessment, not an independent certification. Review the photos, notes and video, and ask about any detail you want to check.

Is every phone PTA approved?

No. The individual listing shows PTA approval and whether the phone is unlocked or JV. Confirm SIM compatibility and applicable registration requirements before purchase.

How do I buy a phone?

Open a phone to see its details, then tap “Ask Shah about this phone.” WhatsApp opens with the phone details included. Shah confirms availability, payment, delivery and any hold directly with you.

What about checking and delivery?

Each listing shows its own checking period: none, 3 days, 7 days or a custom period. Coverage, exchange terms, courier charges and delivery timing are confirmed before purchase. Pickup and Pakistan-wide cash on delivery can be arranged with Shah.

What happens after I request a phone?

We read your request. If we source the phone, it appears in our regular stock. There are no request fees, match guarantees or automatic follow-up messages.
